🛡️ Red-Team Hallucination Defense Methodology

1. Fake Provision Traps

Queries targeting fabricated sections (e.g., Section 99B, Section 3(z), Ayurvedic Patent Free Grant Act) are intercepted by the deterministic statutory parser and safely abstained from.

2. Out-of-Domain Boundaries

Conversational chitchat, weather, movies, coding, or cryptocurrency queries are classified as Out-of-Domain, preventing legal misinformation or off-topic generation.

3. Citation Verification Stripper

The post-generation citation verifier measures n-gram overlap against retrieved statutory chunks. Any claim failing ground-truth alignment has its citation automatically revoked.
